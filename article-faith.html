Why Do People Sometimes Struggle to Maintain Their Faith?

Faith is easy to profess when life is going well. But what happens when prayers seem unanswered, difficulties persist and the future becomes uncertain?

By TOUGHanswers November 2026 6 min read

Many people genuinely believe in God. They pray, attend church, read the Bible and encourage others to trust God. Yet, there are moments when these same people quietly struggle with their own faith.

Sometimes, the struggle comes during a difficult season. At other times, it follows disappointment, unanswered prayers, personal failures or situations that seem impossible to understand.

A person who once prayed confidently may begin to question whether God is listening. Someone who once encouraged others may find it difficult to encourage themselves.

This raises an important question:

"Why do people sometimes struggle to maintain their faith, even when they genuinely believe in God?"

When life does not go as expected

One major reason people struggle with faith is the gap between what they expect and what they experience.

People pray for healing, but the illness continues. They pray for employment, but opportunities do not come. They trust God for a breakthrough, yet their circumstances appear unchanged.

When expectations are not fulfilled within the time they anticipate, disappointment can gradually affect their confidence.

However, a difficult experience does not necessarily mean that God has abandoned someone. Faith is not a guarantee that life will always follow our preferred timetable.

When prayers seem unanswered

Waiting can be one of the most difficult experiences in a person's spiritual journey.

It is one thing to trust God when an answer comes quickly. It is another to continue trusting when months or years pass without seeing the result of a prayer.

During such periods, people may begin to wonder whether they are praying correctly, whether God has heard them or whether something is wrong with their relationship with Him.

It is important to remember that a delay is not necessarily a denial. While waiting, believers can continue to pray, seek wisdom, study God's Word and take responsible steps where necessary.

When disappointment affects spiritual confidence

Sometimes, the struggle is not caused by circumstances alone. It may come from disappointment with people, religious institutions or even personal expectations.

Someone who has been hurt by a trusted spiritual leader may begin to question everything they once believed. Another person may become discouraged after repeated personal failures.

Such experiences can affect spiritual confidence if they are not properly addressed.

People are imperfect, and human failures should not become the only foundation upon which we judge the character of God.

When spiritual nourishment becomes irregular

Faith requires attention and nourishment. Just as the body needs food, rest and care, a person's spiritual life also needs consistent attention.

When prayer, Bible study, fellowship and reflection are continually neglected, spiritual strength may gradually weaken.

This does not mean that every difficult season is caused by a lack of spiritual discipline. However, maintaining healthy spiritual habits can provide strength and direction during challenging times.

Even when motivation is low, small and consistent steps can help a person remain connected to God.

When negative influences become overwhelming

The information people consume and the voices they listen to can influence their thinking.

Constant exposure to fear, discouragement, unhealthy comparisons and messages that undermine their convictions may gradually affect their outlook.

This makes it important to be thoughtful about friendships, conversations, entertainment and the ideas we allow to shape our minds.

Healthy relationships and encouraging spiritual communities can provide support when personal faith is being tested.

How can someone strengthen their faith again?

Struggling with faith does not mean that a person has reached the end of their spiritual journey. It can be an opportunity to examine, rebuild and strengthen their relationship with God.

Some practical steps include:

  • Be honest with God about your questions, fears and disappointments.
  • Return to regular Bible study and prayer, even if you begin with small steps.
  • Seek encouragement from mature and trustworthy believers.
  • Reflect on past experiences where you received help, guidance or strength.
  • Avoid measuring your spiritual journey against another person's experiences.
  • Seek wise counsel when personal struggles become difficult to manage alone.

The Bible offers this encouragement:

"Wait on the LORD: be of good courage, and he shall strengthen thine heart: wait, I say, on the LORD."

Psalm 27:14 (KJV)

Faith does not mean pretending that everything is fine

There is a difference between having faith and pretending that difficulties do not exist.

People of faith can experience sadness, confusion, fear and disappointment. They can have questions and still be sincere in their relationship with God.

What matters is not pretending to be strong at every moment, but continuing to seek God even when the journey becomes difficult.

Faith can grow through seasons of uncertainty when people remain willing to trust, learn and depend on God.

So, what should you do when your faith feels weak?

Do not allow a difficult season to make you conclude that your spiritual journey is over.

Acknowledge what you are experiencing. Identify the things that may be affecting your confidence. Return to the foundations of your faith and seek support from people who can encourage you wisely.

You may not have answers to every question today, but you can continue taking meaningful steps towards spiritual growth.

TOUGHANSWER:

Struggling with faith does not automatically mean that you have stopped believing. Sometimes, it means that you are facing questions, disappointments or pressures that require attention and spiritual strengthening.

Do not confuse a difficult season with the end of your journey. Be honest about your struggles, remain open to growth and keep seeking God, even when the answers are not immediately clear.
