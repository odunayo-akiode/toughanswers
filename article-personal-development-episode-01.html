Why Do Some People Struggle to Discover Their Purpose in Life?

Many people want their lives to count for something but struggle to understand what they are meant to do. Why does finding purpose seem so difficult?

By TOUGHanswers November 2026 5 min read

Have you ever wondered whether you are living the life you were meant to live? You may have a job, responsibilities and ambitions, yet still feel uncertain about your direction.

You are not alone. Many people reach different stages of life still asking: What is my purpose, and what meaningful difference can I make?

What does purpose really mean?

Purpose is the sense of meaning and direction that gives significance to your life. It is not necessarily a particular job, title or financial achievement. It can be expressed through your work, family, relationships, creativity, leadership and service to others.

Why do people struggle to find their purpose?

1. Living by other people's expectations

Family pressure and society's definition of success can lead people to pursue goals that do not reflect their interests, abilities or values.

2. Comparing themselves with others

Constantly measuring your progress against another person's achievements can make you feel unsuccessful. Everyone has a different journey and timeline.

3. Fear of making mistakes

Some people wait for perfect clarity before taking action. But experience, including mistakes, often helps us discover what we are good at and what matters to us.

4. Lack of self-awareness

Without understanding your strengths, interests, values and abilities, it can be difficult to identify a meaningful direction.

5. Equating purpose with money and status

Financial success is important, but it does not automatically provide fulfilment. A meaningful life also involves personal growth and making a positive contribution.

What role do your gifts and abilities play?

Your natural abilities, interests and experiences can offer useful clues. Perhaps you enjoy teaching, solving problems, organising people, creating ideas or helping others.

Ask yourself:

  • What activities do I find meaningful?
  • What abilities do others recognise in me?
  • What problems am I passionate about solving?
  • How can I use my skills to help others?

What role does God play?

For people of faith, discovering purpose also involves considering God's intention for their lives. Ephesians 2:10 describes believers as God's workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works.

Prayer, reflection and studying God's Word can help provide direction. Faith can also inspire us to use our abilities responsibly and serve others. This does not remove the need for practical decisions, learning and hard work.

Practical steps towards discovering your purpose

  • Reflect honestly: Identify your values, interests and ambitions.
  • Develop your strengths: Build your abilities through learning and practice.
  • Help others: Look for problems you can help solve.
  • Take action: Try new opportunities instead of waiting for perfect clarity.
  • Seek guidance: Learn from trusted mentors and experienced people.
  • Be patient: Understand that purpose can become clearer as you grow.

Can your purpose change?

Your values may remain consistent while the way you express your purpose changes. New responsibilities, experiences and opportunities can reveal different ways to make a meaningful contribution.

You do not have to have your entire future figured out before you begin. Start with what you know, use the opportunities available and remain willing to learn.

TOUGHANSWER:

Discovering your purpose is not about copying someone else's success. It is about understanding yourself, developing your abilities and making a meaningful contribution.

For people of faith, it also means seeking God's direction and using their gifts in ways that honour Him and benefit others.

You may not have all the answers today, but you can begin taking purposeful steps. Your journey towards a meaningful life starts with the decisions you make now.
